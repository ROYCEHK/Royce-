# Competitive Intelligence

Owner: Markets & Customers Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Define the competitive set.** Include direct rivals, substitutes, new entrants and customers who are integrating backwards. Tier them: tier 1 is most threatening.
2. **Profile each competitor:**
   - ownership and funding;
   - capacity and its location;
   - products and technology;
   - price points;
   - key customers;
   - cost position (should-cost);
   - strategy statements;
   - recent moves;
   - leadership.
3. **Benchmark** in a side-by-side table with a scorecard, using the dimensions customers actually buy on.
4. **Infer strategy.** Use the 4-corners logic: drivers, current strategy, assumptions and capabilities. From these, predict the likely next moves.
5. **Track signals.** Watch capacity announcements, patents, hires, tenders, pricing, financing and policy support. Grade each signal as confirmed, likely or rumour.

## Rules
- Use public, lawful sources only: filings, press, patents, trade data, conferences, job ads. Never pretext or use confidential third-party data.
- Date every fact and grade its confidence.

## Output
- Competitor one-pagers.
- A benchmark table.
- An inferred-strategy summary.
- A threat ranking.
- A signal watchlist.
