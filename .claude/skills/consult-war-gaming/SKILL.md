# War-Gaming

Owner: Strategy Partner, with the Engagement Sceptic playing the opponents. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Set up.** Name the move to test, the time horizon (usually 2–4 rounds of about 6 months), and the players: our company, 2–4 competitors, key customers, and any regulator or investor.
2. **Build a player profile** for each player:
   - objectives;
   - constraints, such as capacity, cash and politics;
   - capabilities;
   - past behaviour;
   - likely signals.
   Use consult-competitive-intelligence output and public, lawful sources only.
3. **Run the rounds.** In each round, every player states:
   - its move;
   - its rationale;
   - the expected impact on price, volume, share and margin.
   Our team then responds. Keep each player's logic consistent with its profile.
4. **Score the outcome after each round.** Track share, price level, margin and cash for us and for the key rivals.
5. **Extract the insights:**
   - the moves that hurt most;
   - early-warning indicators;
   - pre-emptive actions;
   - triggers for contingency plans;
   - red lines, for example the floor price that would start a price war.

## Output
- A player profile table.
- A round-by-round log.
- An outcome scoreboard.
- The top 5 insights.
- A contingency playbook in the form: trigger → response → owner.
