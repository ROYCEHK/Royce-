# Initiative Prioritisation

Owner: Corporate Finance Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Build an inventory with one row per initiative:**
   - description;
   - owner;
   - value (EBITDA or cash, with a range);
   - cost and capex;
   - FTE and effort;
   - time to impact;
   - dependencies;
   - risk;
   - strategic fit.
   Merge duplicates and split initiatives that are too big.
2. **Set the scoring criteria** and agree the weights before scoring. A typical split:
   - value: 40%;
   - feasibility and effort: 25%;
   - strategic fit: 20%;
   - risk: 15%.
   Score on 1–5 anchored scales, and write down what a 1, a 3 and a 5 mean.
3. **Score**, with the evidence for each score. Flag low-confidence scores.
4. **Plot value against ease** to find:
   - quick wins;
   - big bets;
   - fill-ins;
   - money pits, which should be stopped.
5. **Check against constraints.** Rank by value per unit of the scarce resource (cash, FTE or management attention). Draw the funding line.
6. **Sequence.** Order by dependencies, capacity per quarter and early wins for momentum. Hand the result to consult-transformation-roadmap.

## Output
- A scored initiative list.
- A 2×2 chart.
- The funded and unfunded lists.
- A stop list.
- Sequencing notes.
- A sensitivity check: does the top 5 change if the weights shift by ±10%?
