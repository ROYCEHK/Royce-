# Customer Segmentation

Owner: Markets & Customers Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Pick the segmentation basis** that drives a different action:
   - needs or buying criteria;
   - application;
   - behaviour (volume, loyalty, switching);
   - value (margin, cost-to-serve).
   Firmographics such as industry and size are descriptors, not the segmentation itself.
2. **Gather the data:**
   - CRM and sales history;
   - customer profitability (cost-to-serve);
   - interviews and Voice of Customer;
   - tender records.
3. **Build 4–7 segments.** Each must be distinct, sizeable, reachable and stable. Name each segment by its need ("Performance-critical cell makers"), not by a letter.
4. **Profile each segment:**
   - size and growth;
   - share of wallet;
   - margin;
   - key buying criteria and their weights;
   - decision-making unit;
   - competitive intensity.
5. **Prioritise** on an attractiveness × ability-to-win matrix.
6. **Set the value proposition and go-to-market per segment:**
   - offer;
   - pricing model;
   - channel;
   - service level;
   - account coverage (key account, distributor or digital).

## Output
- A segment definitions table.
- Segment sizes and economics.
- A prioritisation matrix.
- A value-proposition canvas for each priority segment.
- GTM implications.
- A list of customers assigned to segments, where data exists.
