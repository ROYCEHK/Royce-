# Assumption Audit

Owner: Strategy Partner, with the Engagement Sceptic as challenger. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Extract the assumptions** from the document. Include the implicit ones: market growth, share, price, cost curve, timing, competitor reaction, regulation, execution capacity and funding.
2. **Make each one explicit and measurable.** For example, "Demand grows" becomes "EV-battery SWCNT demand grows ≥30% CAGR 2026–30".
3. **Rate each assumption** on three scales:
   - Evidence: H, M or L, with the source.
   - Sensitivity: the value impact if the assumption is wrong by a plausible margin. Run a tornado if a model exists.
   - Controllability: internal or external.
4. **Classify** into a 2×2 of evidence against impact. Assumptions with low evidence and high impact are the **critical assumptions**.
5. **Design tests** for each critical assumption: the cheapest, fastest data, experiment or expert call. Set a kill or continue threshold, a signpost, and the date to check it.
6. **Reverse-engineer.** Ask what has to be true for the plan to hit its target, and whether that is believable.

## Output
- An assumption register in the form: # | assumption | value | evidence | source | sensitivity | critical? | test | signpost | owner.
- The top 5 critical assumptions, with a one-line recommendation each.
