# Narrative Builder

Owner: Communications & Decisions Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Know the audience.** Identify who they are, what they already believe, the decision you want from them, and how much time they have.
2. **Write the answer first.** Put the governing thought in one sentence, as a recommendation rather than a topic.
3. **Pyramid.** Support the answer with 3–5 key arguments that are MECE and connected either inductively (grouped) or deductively (a chain). Each argument is backed by evidence.
4. **Opening.** Use SCR in a few lines (situation, complication, question), leading straight into the answer.
5. **Ghost deck.** Storyboard it before making any slides. Each slide is one action title, a full-sentence takeaway of 15 words or fewer, and a sketch of the chart that proves it. Read only the titles: they must tell the whole story on their own (horizontal logic). Each slide's body must prove its title (vertical logic).
6. **Executive summary.** One page: the answer, the key arguments with their numbers, the ask, and the next steps.
7. **Tighten.** Cut every slide that does not support the answer and move it to the appendix. Replace adjectives with numbers.

## Output
- The governing thought.
- The pyramid outline.
- A ghost deck table in the form: # | action title | evidence/chart | source.
- The executive summary.
- An appendix list.
