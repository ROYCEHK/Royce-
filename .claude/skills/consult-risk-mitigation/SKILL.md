# Risk Mitigation

Owner: Operations & Transformation Partner, with the Engagement Sceptic leading the pre-mortem. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Identify the risks** across these categories, checked in a MECE way:
   - strategic;
   - market and commercial;
   - operational and supply;
   - financial (FX, liquidity, credit);
   - legal and regulatory;
   - technology and cyber;
   - people;
   - reputational and ESG;
   - geopolitical.
2. **Pre-mortem.** Write "It is 18 months later and this failed. Why?" and list 10 or more causes. Add the new ones to the register.
3. **Assess each risk:**
   - likelihood, on a 1–5 scale;
   - impact, on a 1–5 scale, with the anchors defined in USD or in days of delay;
   - velocity;
   - the current controls.
   Plot the results on a heatmap.
4. **Choose a response** for each top risk: avoid, reduce, transfer (insurance or contract) or accept. Specify the mitigation actions, owner, cost and due date. The residual score comes after the mitigation.
5. **Set triggers and contingency plans.** Each top risk gets early-warning indicators with thresholds, and a pre-agreed contingency action for when a threshold is breached.
6. **Monitoring.** Set a review cadence, and link the triggers to consult-kpi-architecture.

## Output
- A risk register in the form: # | risk | cause | effect | L | I | score | controls | response | actions | owner | trigger | residual.
- A heatmap.
- The top 10 risks, with their mitigation plans.

Legal, tax, safety and regulatory sign-off stays with qualified people.
