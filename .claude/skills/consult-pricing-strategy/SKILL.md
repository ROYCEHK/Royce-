# Pricing Strategy

Owner: Markets & Customers Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Diagnose the current price:**
   - **Price waterfall:** list price → on-invoice discounts → off-invoice items (rebates, freight, payment terms, free samples) → pocket price. Find the leakage.
   - **Price dispersion:** compare similar customers and volumes. Explain the outliers.
2. **Find the value to the customer.** Calculate the economic value to the customer (EVC): the next-best alternative's price plus the differentiation value. For example, the cost saved through lower loading, gains in performance or cycle life, and process savings. Quantify this per unit of the customer's output, such as per kWh.
3. **Set out the pricing options:**
   - level;
   - structure (per kg, per kWh, tiered, volume-banded, index-linked to inputs);
   - architecture (good, better, best grades);
   - terms.
4. **Predict the competitive response.** Ask how rivals and customers will react, and run a quick war-game for big moves. Set a floor price and walk-away rules.
5. **Implement:**
   - account-by-account targets;
   - negotiation guidance and scripts;
   - discount governance and an approval matrix;
   - tracking KPIs (realised price, leakage %, win rate).

## Output
- A waterfall and leakage estimate.
- An EVC model.
- A recommended price architecture.
- The price-change plan, with its revenue and margin impact and the volume-risk sensitivity.
- A governance rules table.
