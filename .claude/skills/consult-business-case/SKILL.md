# Business Case

Owner: Corporate Finance Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Frame the case.** State the decision, the alternatives (including do-nothing), the time horizon, the discount rate or WACC with its source, and the currency and FX assumptions.
2. **Base case vs. case.** Model the incremental cash flows only:
   - revenue drivers;
   - cost and margin;
   - capex;
   - working capital;
   - tax;
   - terminal value, if justified.
   Every driver needs a source or an explicitly labelled estimate.
3. **Calculate the metrics:**
   - NPV;
   - IRR;
   - payback;
   - peak funding need;
   - EBITDA and EPS impact;
   - ROIC against WACC.
4. **Scenarios and sensitivity:**
   - base, upside and downside cases, using drivers from consult-assumption-audit;
   - a tornado chart;
   - break-even values, such as the price or volume at which NPV = 0.
5. **Risks and mitigation.** Use consult-risk-mitigation. Show the risk-adjusted value where it matters.
6. **Non-financial value.** Cover strategic fit, optionality and ESG, and keep these separate from the NPV.
7. **Ask and plan.** State the funding request, the stage gates, the KPIs and the post-investment review date.

## Output
- A one-page case summary.
- A model in xlsx, using the xlsx skill. Run audit-xls before release.
- Scenario and sensitivity tables.
- A risk register excerpt.
- The recommendation and its conditions.
