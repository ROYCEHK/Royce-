# Profit Pool Analysis

Owner: Markets & Customers Partner, with the Corporate Finance Partner checking the margins. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Define the pool boundary.** Set out the value-chain steps (e.g. raw materials → CNT synthesis → dispersion → electrode → cell → pack → OEM), the regions and the year.
2. **Estimate revenue** for each step and segment, using the market-mapping outputs.
3. **Estimate margin** for each step from listed-peer financials (EBIT or EBITDA margin), annual reports, analyst reports and expert input. Then compute profit = revenue × margin.
4. **Draw the profit-pool map.** The x-axis is the share of industry revenue and the y-axis is the margin, so each block's area is its profit. Highlight where profit share far exceeds revenue share.
5. **Explain the pools.** Ask why each pool is rich or poor: concentration, switching costs, IP, scarcity, regulation.
6. **Shift analysis.** Ask how the pools will move in 3–5 years: commoditisation, integration, new tech, policy.
7. **Implications.** Look for moves that capture more profit: integrate forward or back, a new business model, a segment shift, value-based pricing.

## Output
- A profit-pool table and chart, with data sources and confidence for each step.
- The drivers for each pool.
- A future-shift view.
- The top 3 value-capture moves, handed to consult-strategic-options.
