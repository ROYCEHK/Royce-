# Situation Assessment

Owner: Strategy Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Performance baseline.** Cover 3–5 years plus the current year: revenue, volume, price, margin, cash and ROIC. Show the trend, the CAGR and the variance against plan.
2. **Driver decomposition.** Split results into price, volume, mix, cost and FX bridges. Identify the 2–3 drivers behind 80% of the change.
3. **External lens.** Use PESTEL and an industry structure scan (Porter five forces) for the forces acting on the business. Hand deeper market work to consult-market-mapping.
4. **Internal lens.** Assess capabilities, assets, cost position and organisation, and state where the business is advantaged or disadvantaged.
5. **Root causes.** Separate symptoms from causes with a "5 whys" chain on each problem area.
6. **SCR summary:**
   - Situation: what is stable and agreed.
   - Complication: what changed, and the threat.
   - Resolution question: this feeds problem framing.

## Output
- A one-page SCR.
- A baseline fact pack: tables carrying action titles, with sources.
- A root-cause map.
- A list of "what we don't know yet" items, each with an owner.
