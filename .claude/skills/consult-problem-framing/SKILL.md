# Problem-Framing

Owner: Strategy Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Restate the trigger.** Say what happened and why it matters now: the burning platform or the opportunity.
2. **Write the key question** so it is SMART: specific, measurable, action-oriented, relevant and time-bound. For example: "How can OCSiAl Asia raise battery-segment gross margin from X% to Y% by Q4 2027 without losing top-5 accounts?"
3. **Fill the Problem Statement Worksheet:**
   - Context
   - Criteria for success, quantified
   - Scope: in and out
   - Constraints: money, time, people, policy
   - Stakeholders, and who decides
   - Key sources of insight
4. **Draw the issue tree.** Keep it MECE and 2–3 levels deep. Choose the tree type deliberately:
   - **diagnostic** ("why?"), driver-based, e.g. profit = volume × price − cost;
   - **solution** ("how?"), levers.
5. **Turn each leaf into a hypothesis.** Each one needs a test that could prove it false and the data that test needs.
6. **Prioritise the leaves** by impact × ease of testing. Hand the top 3–5 to the workplan.

## Output
- A Problem Statement (one page).
- An issue tree, as an indented list or mermaid.
- A hypothesis list in the form: hypothesis | test | data | owner.
- Open questions for Royce. Ask at most 3, and only where the answer changes the scope.

## Pitfalls to check
- The question is really a solution in disguise ("should we build plant X?" when the real problem is supply security).
- The scope is too wide to finish by the deadline.
- No one decision-maker is named.
