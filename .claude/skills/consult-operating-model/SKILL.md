# Operating Model Design

Owner: Operations & Transformation Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Set the design principles from the strategy**, 5–8 of them. For example: "regional P&L ownership for Asia", "global standard processes on SAP S/4HANA", "lean HQ". They are the yardstick for every choice that follows.
2. **Map the current model:**
   - organisation chart and spans and layers;
   - key processes;
   - decision rights;
   - governance forums;
   - cost and FTE by function and location;
   - the pain points.
3. **Design across the dimensions:**
   - structure (functional, regional, BU or matrix);
   - roles;
   - decision rights (a RACI or RAPID matrix for the top 15–20 decisions);
   - processes;
   - governance and cadence;
   - performance management;
   - capabilities and talent;
   - technology and data;
   - footprint and sourcing (in-house, shared service centre, outsourced).
4. **Generate 2–3 design options.** Score them against the principles, cost, risk and ease of transition.
5. **Detail the chosen model:**
   - the top 2 levels of the organisation;
   - mandates;
   - interfaces;
   - the cost and FTE impact;
   - the transition plan and the change risks.

## Output
- The design principles.
- A current-state diagnosis.
- An options assessment.
- The target operating model on one page.
- A decision-rights matrix.
- A cost and FTE bridge.
- Transition steps, handed to consult-transformation-roadmap.
