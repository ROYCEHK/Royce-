# Market Mapping

Owner: Markets & Customers Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Define the market precisely.** Name the product, the application, the geography, the unit (volume and value) and the base year. Most sizing errors are definition errors.
2. **Segment the map in a MECE way** by application or end-market, customer type, region and technology. Show it as a market map with sizes in each cell.
3. **Size the market.** Use at least 2 methods and reconcile them:
   - **Top-down:** take the end-market volume, multiply by penetration, then by loading (intensity), then by price.
   - **Bottom-up:** sum customers or plants, multiplied by consumption and price.
   - **Supply-side:** sum producer capacity, multiplied by utilisation.
   If the methods differ by more than 20%, explain why.
4. **Set out the growth drivers** by segment, with a CAGR. Run base, bull and bear scenarios, and name the explicit drivers and their signposts.
5. **Map the value chain.** Show who captures each step, the concentration at each step, and where the bargaining power sits.
6. **Show TAM, SAM and SOM**, with the logic for each filter.

## Output
- A market definition.
- A market map: a table or chart with action titles.
- A sizing model with the methods side by side.
- A growth scenario table.
- A value-chain map.
- A sources list.
