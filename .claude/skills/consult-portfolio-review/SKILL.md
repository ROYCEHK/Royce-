# Portfolio Review

Owner: Corporate Finance Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **Define the units.** These are business units, products, grades, regions, plants or investments. Use consistent periods and allocate costs consistently. State the allocation rules.
2. **Build the fact base for each unit:**
   - revenue and growth;
   - gross margin and EBITDA;
   - capital employed;
   - ROIC against WACC (economic profit);
   - cash conversion;
   - market growth and relative share.
3. **Position the units on three views:**
   - growth against ROIC, sized by capital;
   - market attractiveness against competitive strength (GE-McKinsey-style 9-box);
   - a value creation waterfall.
4. **Assess fit and parenting.** Ask whether we are the best owner, and test the synergies, shared capabilities and strategic role (core, growth, option, non-core).
5. **Assign a decision to each unit:** invest to grow, fix (with a margin or turnaround plan), harvest for cash, or exit or partner. Capital reallocation should follow value creation, not history.
6. **Portfolio-level view.** Show the balance of risk, cash generation and growth, and the gaps the portfolio should fill.

## Output
- A portfolio fact table.
- Positioning charts.
- Unit-by-unit verdicts with rationale.
- A capital reallocation proposal.
- The value at stake.
