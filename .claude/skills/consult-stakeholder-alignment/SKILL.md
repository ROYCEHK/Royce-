# Stakeholder Alignment

Owner: Communications & Decisions Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Method
1. **List the stakeholders.** Include:
   - the decision-makers;
   - the influencers;
   - the people who implement;
   - anyone affected;
   - potential blockers, both internal (Group CEO and CFO, board, BU heads) and external (investors, partners, regulators).
2. **Map them.** Place each on a grid of influence against current stance (champion, supporter, neutral, sceptic, blocker). Also note their interest in the decision and the relationships between them: who listens to whom.
3. **Understand each key stakeholder:**
   - what they care about and their KPIs;
   - what they fear losing;
   - the objections they will raise;
   - the evidence that would move them;
   - the best messenger and the best channel.
4. **Set a target stance.** Decide the stance each person needs by the decision date, and the gap from where they are now.
5. **Engagement plan.** List each one-to-one pre-wire: who, by whom, message, ask, date. Sequence them so that champions are engaged first and blockers are never surprised in the room.
6. **Tailor the messages.** Express the same core answer in each stakeholder's language, handing this to consult-narrative-builder.
7. **Track.** After each touchpoint, update each person's stance and log the commitments.

## Output
- A stakeholder map.
- Interest and objection profiles.
- The engagement plan.
- Pre-wire talking points.
- A stance tracker.

For drafted messages, follow the ocsial-command email rules: drafts go to Royce only, and nothing is ever sent.
