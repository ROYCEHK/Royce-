# Decision Memo

Owner: Communications & Decisions Partner. Follow consult-operating-system.

## Template (two pages at most; one page is better)
```
DECISION MEMO – <title>                         Date · Author · Decision-maker
1. Decision required   One sentence, the yes/no or choice, and the deadline.
2. Recommendation      The answer and the 2–3 reasons, with numbers.
3. Background          Three lines of context (SCR).
4. Options considered  A table: option | value | cost | risk | why rejected or chosen (include do-nothing).
5. Financials          NPV/IRR/payback or P&L impact, funding, key assumptions (link the model).
6. Risks & mitigations The top 3–5, plus signposts that would reverse the decision.
7. Stakeholder view    Who supports, who has concerns, and how each is addressed.
8. Next steps          Action | owner | date, including the post-decision review date.
Appendix: sources and the assumptions register.
```

## Rules
- Use active voice and no jargon. Every claim carries its number and source.
- Present the options fairly, including the strongest case against the recommendation.
- If the evidence is weak, say so and propose a smaller reversible step.
- When the decision is made, log it in the Decision Log (Chief of Staff) and the Engagement Register.
